## Class Features

- **Dueling:** +2 damage with a one-handed melee weapon; shield is allowed.
- **Second Wind:** 2 uses; Bonus Action to regain 1d10+2 HP.
- **Action Surge:** 1/Short or Long Rest; gain one extra Action (not Magic).
- **Tactical Mind:** after a failed ability check, spend Second Wind to add 1d10; if still failed, the use is not spent.
- **Savage Attacker:** once per turn on a weapon hit, roll weapon damage dice twice and use either roll.
- Fighter subclass unlocks at level 3.
