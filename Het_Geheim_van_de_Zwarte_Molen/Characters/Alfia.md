## Class Features

- **Sneak Attack 1d6:** once per turn on a qualifying Finesse/Ranged hit.
- **Cunning Action:** Bonus Action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Expertise:** Sleight of Hand and Stealth.
- **Thieves' Cant:** secret cant plus one extra language.
- **Alert:** add Proficiency to Initiative; after rolling, Initiative can be swapped with a willing ally.
- Rogue subclass unlocks at level 3.

## Species Traits

- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Gnomish Cunning:** Advantage on INT, WIS, and CHA saving throws.
- **Rock Gnome:** Mending + Prestidigitation; INT is the lineage spellcasting ability.
- **Clockwork Device:** spend 10 minutes with Prestidigitation to create a Tiny device; up to 3 at once.
